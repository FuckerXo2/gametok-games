Age Rating Compliance (4.7.5)

All games in GameTOK are rated 4+ (suitable for all ages). Games are reviewed before being added to ensure they:

  • Contain no violence, gore, or mature themes
  • Have no gambling or simulated gambling
  • Include no sexual content or nudity
  • Are free from profanity or crude humor

If a game is found to exceed the 4+ rating, it is immediately removed from the platform.

Content Review Process

All games are reviewed before being added to ensure they contain no objectionable content, are age-appropriate, and function correctly.

GameMonetize Partnership

GameTOK is a registered publisher on GameMonetize, a legitimate HTML5 game distribution platform used by thousands of publishers worldwide. GameMonetize games are pre-screened by their platform for quality and content compliance.
