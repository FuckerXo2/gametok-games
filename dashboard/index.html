GameTok Bridge
Full guide (skill.md)

Connect your web game (HTML5, Canvas, Three.js, Phaser, Godot) to GameTok with one script tag. Your game stays on your own hosting.

1. Add the bridge to your index.html
<script src="https://gametok.co/bridge.js"></script>
2. Scores & game over (optional)
if (window.GameTok) { window.GameTok.submitScore(score); window.GameTok.gameOver(score); }
